The journey
A sketch in the browser. Click the map to jump.
One zip: the whole journey as one MIDI file, a folder per chapter with a file per layer, and a rack card that tells you which plugin goes on which track.
How it works
  1. Tick your plugins in Your rack. The machine only chooses from your ticks.
  2. The oracle gives five words. Click a word to roll it again, lock the ones you love.
  3. Deep journey is one long arc through all chapters. Mixed journey makes every chapter a song of its own, with its own build, drop and break, mixed into the next.
  4. Every word becomes a chapter: a leading machine, guests, a feel, a key. Change anything on the cards, or roll it.
  5. Listen to the sketch in the browser. It plays the journey with simple sounds, your plugins will sound far better.
  6. Make the journey. Open Rack_Card.txt, load the plugins it names, and drop every file on bar 1.
