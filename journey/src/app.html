One zip: the whole journey as one MIDI file, a folder per chapter with a file per layer, your records each on their own, and a rack card that tells you which plugin goes on which track.
How it works
  1. Tick your plugins in Your rack. The machine only chooses from your ticks.
  2. The oracle gives five words. Click a word to roll it again, lock the ones you love.
  3. Deep journey is one long arc through all chapters. Mixed journey makes every chapter a song of its own, with its own build, drop and break, mixed into the next.
  4. Every word becomes a chapter: a leading machine, guests, a feel, a key. Change anything on the cards, or roll it.
  5. Listen to the sketch in the browser. It plays the journey with simple sounds, your plugins will sound far better.
  6. Your records: press your own records from one to three styles, as many as you like. The story decides which chapter plays which record and mixes them inside the chapter. Every record also comes out on its own in the Records folder.
  7. Built your DAW from a result? Choose My template and load that MIDI: every new journey lands on exactly those tracks.
  8. Name your journey, or let the dice suggest a name, and press Make it. Open Rack_Card.txt, load the plugins it names, and drop every file on bar 1.
