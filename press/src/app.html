How it works
  1. Tick your plugins in Your rack. The press only chooses from your ticks.
  2. Choose one to three styles for your record. Orchestra lets the mood choose the orchestral form. Two Studio styles: the calmer one carries the harmony, the faster one the beat.
  3. Two Studio styles: choose under Beat which one plays the beat. It also sets the tempo, the other style carries the harmony.
  4. Room for a voice: the singer owns verse and chorus. The melody plays a soft countermelody under her and its own hook in the intro and the breaks, the harmony sits low and soft, the orchestra plays with three strings.
  5. Click the mood word to roll it. It gives the record its character, its colour and its key.
  6. Side B is a full remix: the same motif and key, with a new style, a new form, and your hints.
  7. The record pictures are drawn from the notes: every ring is eight bars, the dots are the beat, the arcs are the harmony, the line is the melody. Choose the colour you like.
  8. Listen to the sketch, then press it. Open Rack_Card.txt, load the plugins it names, and drop the files on bar 1.
  9. Your own track: load a WAV or MP3. The press listens to it in your browser, draws its record picture, and presses two remixes of it as MIDI, with its tempo, its key, its length and a motif from its melody. Choose the styles and hints for the remixes as usual.
  10. Built your DAW from a record? Choose My template and load that MIDI: every new record lands on exactly those tracks, side A and side B.
