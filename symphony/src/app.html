Into your DAW with any synth
  1. Drag the full MIDI file into your song. Every part lands on its own track.
  2. Put any synth or sampler on any track: a 303, a pad, a piano, your choice.
  3. No keyswitches and no CC1 in these files. Dynamics live in the velocity, CC11 is expression.
  4. Rubato is baked into the note timing. Keep the tempo of your song at the tempo shown here.
Into your DAW with BBC Discover
  1. Drag the full MIDI file into your song. Every instrument lands on its own track.
  2. On each track, load BBC Symphony Orchestra Discover and pick the instrument with the same name.
  3. Leave the keyswitches as they are: C-2 Long, C#-2 Spiccato or Short, D-2 Pizzicato, D#-2 Tremolo.
  4. CC1 drives the dynamics and CC11 the expression. They are already written in.
  5. The first bar is empty on purpose, so the keyswitches and the early long notes have room.
  6. Rubato is baked into the note timing. Keep the tempo of your song at the tempo shown here.
  7. Any other sound works too: drag your own plugin onto a track and ignore the low keyswitch notes.
